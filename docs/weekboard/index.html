To schedule

Every week

Practice, school, lifts and work that repeat. They show up on every week automatically, and Auto-fit plans homework around them.

Your day

Auto-fit only uses time between school and 30 minutes before bed on weekdays, and from your weekend start time on Saturday and Sunday. It leaves a 15-minute break after practice and games.

How to use it

  • Tap ＋ to add homework, practice, games, work or anything else.
  • Homework with a due date goes in To schedule. Tap Auto-fit and it finds time before it's due.
  • Press and hold anything for a moment, then drag it to another day. Or use the day buttons.
  • Tap the box to check it off. Tap the item to edit it.
