How to Play

The job

You run one four-way intersection for exactly 20 seconds. Move as many cars through as you can without a collision.

Traffic builds every 5 seconds: light, moderate, heavy, then rush hour.

Two phases

A gives green to north and south. D gives green to east and west.

You can also tap the signal heads on the corners or the big buttons under the map.

Safe switching

Every switch runs yellow, then all-red, then the new green. Both directions can never be green together.

Each switch costs time where nobody moves, so switching too often wastes the clock.

Collisions

Drivers close to the line keep going on yellow. Distracted and aggressive drivers push it further. Trucks and buses are slow to clear the box.

Switch when the green approach has a gap, not while a platoon is rolling in.

Scoring

+100 per car cleared, multiplied by your combo (up to x3). +25 for a car that never had to stop. Combo milestones every 10 cars pay a bonus.

Collision: −500 and your combo resets. The combo also breaks if no car clears for about 2.5 seconds, a driver honks, or the intersection hits gridlock.

Levels

Ten handcrafted shifts, each with a twist: one-way floods, closed lanes, ambulances, rain, trucks, street racers and a broken controller. Each has three star goals.

Ambulances flash red and blue. Get them through fast for +400. If one waits over 2.5 seconds it costs −300.

Congestion

Long queues fill the meter. Above 80% it is gridlock: your combo breaks and you lose 100 points a second. A driver who waits too long honks for −40.

At time zero, flow efficiency and a clean-shift bonus are added to your score.
