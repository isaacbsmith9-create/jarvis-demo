Hook rules this app follows

  • The hook lands in the first 2 seconds, before people swipe.
  • About 2.5 spoken words per second, so a 30-second video is about 75 words.
  • One idea per video, short sentences, and on-screen text for people watching muted.
  • Open a loop early ("the third one changed everything") and pay it off at the end.
