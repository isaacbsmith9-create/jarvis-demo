Mission brief · Sector 7

The mining drones went rogue. Hold the extraction beacon for 8 waves.

Your three mechs fire on their own. Your job is everything else: where they stand, when they cool down, and who gets the ammo.

  • Heat builds every time a mech fires. At 100 it overheats: shields drop to zero and its fire rate halves until it cools below 35.
  • Vent dumps heat fast, but the mech can't shoot for 3 seconds.
  • Rubble tiles give cover: 35% less damage. Walls block line of fire, except for Longbow's artillery shells.
  • The rear extraction zone (striped amber) cools mechs faster, patches hulls and reloads ammo from your salvage reserve.
  • Scrapped drones add salvage. Spend it with Allocate ammo.
