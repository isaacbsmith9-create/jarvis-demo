How these games work

  • A live defender beats cones. Every rep has a real read, so the move you learn is the one that works in a game.
  • The rules teach the skill. Want more paint touches? Make paint finishes worth more. Want quicker decisions? Shrink the dribbles or the clock.
  • Always keep score. Competing makes the reps game speed and shows you what is actually working.
  • Short and intense. Games to 5 or 7 with quick rotations. Quality over volume.
  • Even it out. If one player is better, give the other a scoring bonus so every game stays close.
